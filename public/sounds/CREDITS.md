# Sounds

The fighting's sounds, converted to mono MP3 (64 kbit/s) with ffmpeg so that every browser plays them (Safari
among them); otherwise unchanged. Played by `src/ui/sound.ts`, a few takes for each kind at a slightly varied
pitch.

- `impact_metal_*`, `impact_plate_*` (blades on blades and on plate), `impact_soft_*`, `impact_punch_*` (blows,
  falls), `impact_mining_*` (stone breaking: a ball landing, a wall falling, and slowed down, a gun's crack) — from
  **"Impact Sounds"** by **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), licensed **CC0 1.0** (public domain), as packed in
  github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-impact-sounds-for-godot.

Kenney's "RPG Audio" (CC0, github.com/Boyquotes/kenney-rpg-audio-for-godot) was looked through and is not used yet.
